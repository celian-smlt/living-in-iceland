05 · Doing business

Small country, short network

  • Decision-makers are easy to reach, and often sit in the first meeting.
  • Business and social life mix: dinners, after-work drinks, the hot tub.
  • Your word is your reputation: in a market this small, everyone hears about a broken promise.

Sources: US Trade.gov Country Commercial Guide (business travel), Pebl and Rivermate business culture guides

05 · Doing business

A meeting in Reykjavík: short and to the point

  1. 01

    A little small talk

    The weather, your trip. A few minutes, then straight to business.

  2. 02

    Get to the point

    A realistic offer, backed by facts. No hard sell, no overpromising.

  3. 03

    Decide together

    The boss leads the discussion, the team has a say. Decisions can be fast.

  4. 04

    Follow up in writing

    A short, plain e-mail: who does what, and by when.

07 · The verdict

Should you pack your bags?

Go for it

  • Very high wages, strong unions
  • Open to EU workers, low unemployment
  • Safe, green, stunning nature
  • English widely spoken

Think twice

  • Rent eats about 40% of take-home pay
  • High taxes and prices
  • Dark winters, harsh weather
  • Icelandic needed for many jobs

Iceland pays well, if you can handle the price tag and the winter.
